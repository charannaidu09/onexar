How do I open a new trading account?

Opening an account is quick and easy. Click “Open live account”, complete the registration form, verify your identity (KYC) and fund your account to start trading.

What types of trading accounts does Onexar offer?

We offer ECN, Pro, Standard and Swap-Free accounts, each designed to suit a different trading style. Customised accounts are available for institutional traders.

How can I deposit and withdraw funds?

You can fund your account and withdraw by bank wire, credit card, crypto or escrow services.

Is Onexar a regulated forex broker?

Yes. ONEXAR LTD holds an International Brokerage & Clearing House Licence in Comoros, company number 15988, licence number L15988/OL.

Does Onexar allow social trading and managed accounts?

Yes. You can copy experienced traders through Copy Trading, or have your funds managed through PAMM and MAM accounts.
